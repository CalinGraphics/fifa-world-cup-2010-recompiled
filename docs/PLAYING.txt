2010 FIFA World Cup South Africa: Recompiled
============================================

An unofficial native PC port of the Xbox 360 game. No game files are included:
you need your own Xbox 360 copy of the game (Europe disc, En/Es/Nl).

Getting started
---------------
1. Run Launcher.exe.
2. Click "Disc image (.iso)" and pick your Xbox 360 disc image
   (or "Folder" for a folder that holds default.xex and fifadllzf.xex).
   The launcher checks that it is the right version.
3. Choose your settings and press PLAY.

Settings
--------
Render resolution  720p is the original. 1440p (2x) and 4K (3x) render the game
                   at a higher resolution for a sharper picture.
Texture filtering  Anisotropic filtering keeps the pitch and stands sharp at an angle.
Controls           Controller (Xbox / any XInput pad) or Keyboard.
V-Sync             On by default.

Keyboard controls (when Controls = Keyboard)
--------------------------------------------
Move           W A S D
A / B / X / Y  Space / Backspace / L / P
LB / RB        1 / 3
LT / RT        Q / E
Right stick    Arrow keys
D-pad          Shift + Arrow keys
Start / Back   Enter / Tab

Other keys
----------
F3       performance overlay
Alt+F4   quit

Files
-----
Settings, saves, logs and the shader cache go to
%LOCALAPPDATA%\FIFA World Cup 2010 Recompiled
(create an empty portable.txt next to Launcher.exe to keep them in a "userdata"
folder here instead). "Saves and logs folder" in the launcher opens it.

Requirements
------------
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a Direct3D 12 graphics card with current drivers.
